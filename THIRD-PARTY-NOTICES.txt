FluxMedia.Playback third-party notices
=======================================

This package bundles managed third-party components required by the
playback SDK. It does not distribute FFmpeg native DLLs, ffmpeg.exe,
or ffprobe.exe. Applications must provide compatible FFmpeg native shared
libraries separately.
